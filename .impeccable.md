## Design Context

### Users
Friends, gaming communities, and creators who want to make and share tier lists quickly in a fun social context.

### Brand Personality
Loud, competitive, arcade-slick. The energy of a fighting-game character-select screen or a Persona 5 menu — confident, kinetic, a little aggressive. Never corporate, never pastel, never soft SaaS.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark theme (used at night, next to a running game; dark is the correct context call)
- Near-black ink surfaces tinted toward orange, one crimson-orange accent used sparingly but hard
- Display type: Anton (condensed poster caps). Body/UI: Barlow
- Anti-references: generic SaaS landing pages, glassmorphism, purple-blue gradients

### Design Principles
- Prioritize playful energy and social-share vibes over sterile productivity visuals.
- Keep contrast high and hierarchy bold for fast scanability during ranking.
- Use orange as the dominant action/accent color with restrained supporting neutrals.
- Type does the decorating — huge condensed caps instead of icons and illustrations.
- Dark surfaces get depth from lightness steps, never from glow or blur
- Preserve responsiveness and interaction clarity while adding personality.
- Avoid generic glassmorphism, gradient-text gimmicks, and template-like hero blocks.
